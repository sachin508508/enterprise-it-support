# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 1. Company Overview

### 1.1 About Nexora Technologies

Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional software and IT services company headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.

The company provides technology solutions to businesses across different industries. Nexora focuses on software development, cloud services, data engineering, artificial intelligence, application development, and technology consulting.

Nexora was established in 2018 with the objective of helping organizations use modern technology to improve their business operations.

The company has more than 350 employees working across multiple teams and locations.

Nexora follows a hybrid working model that allows employees to work from office and remotely according to their team arrangements.

### 1.2 Company Locations

Nexora Technologies operates from the following locations:

- Chennai, Tamil Nadu — Headquarters
- Bengaluru, Karnataka — Technology and Engineering Office
- Hyderabad, Telangana — Technology and Operations Office

The Chennai office serves as the company's primary headquarters and houses corporate, engineering, IT, HR, finance, and management teams.

The Bengaluru office primarily supports software engineering, data, and AI teams.

The Hyderabad office supports engineering, cloud operations, and business operations.

### 1.3 Business Areas

Nexora provides services in the following areas:

- Software development
- Web and application development
- Cloud infrastructure and operations
- Data engineering
- Artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Quality assurance and software testing
- Technology consulting
- Business application support

Different teams within the company may work on different customer projects and internal initiatives.

### 1.4 Company Working Model

Nexora follows a hybrid working model.

Employees may work from a company office or an approved remote location depending on their role, team requirements, project requirements, and manager-approved working arrangements.

Employees are expected to remain available during their assigned working hours regardless of whether they are working from an office or an approved remote location.

## 2. Company Mission

### 2.1 Mission Statement

Nexora Technologies aims to help organizations solve business problems through reliable, scalable, and practical technology solutions.

The company focuses on combining software engineering, cloud technologies, data, and artificial intelligence to create solutions that provide measurable value to customers.

### 2.2 Company Objectives

Nexora's primary objectives are:

1. Deliver reliable technology solutions to customers.
2. Maintain high standards of software quality.
3. Encourage innovation and continuous improvement.
4. Develop employee technical and professional skills.
5. Protect company and customer information.
6. Promote collaboration across teams.
7. Build long-term relationships with customers and business partners.
8. Maintain a professional and inclusive workplace.

Employees are expected to understand how their responsibilities contribute to these objectives.

## 3. Company Values

Nexora Technologies follows six core values.

### 3.1 Customer Focus

Employees should understand customer requirements and work toward delivering solutions that meet agreed business and technical expectations.

Customer information, requirements, feedback, and project information should be handled responsibly.

### 3.2 Innovation

Nexora encourages employees to explore new ideas, technologies, and approaches that can improve products, services, and internal processes.

Innovation should be balanced with reliability, security, business requirements, and practical implementation.

### 3.3 Integrity

Employees are expected to communicate honestly, follow company rules, and take responsibility for their decisions and actions.

### 3.4 Collaboration

Employees are expected to work effectively with colleagues, managers, customers, and other stakeholders.

Teams should share relevant information and cooperate when responsibilities overlap.

### 3.5 Accountability

Employees are responsible for completing assigned work, communicating progress, meeting agreed expectations, and raising issues when they cannot complete a responsibility as planned.

### 3.6 Continuous Learning

Technology changes continuously. Nexora encourages employees to develop their technical knowledge, business understanding, communication skills, and professional capabilities.

Employees are encouraged to participate in training, knowledge-sharing sessions, workshops, and other approved learning activities.

## 5. Working Hours

### 5.1 Standard Working Schedule

The standard working schedule at Nexora Technologies is:

- Working days: Monday to Friday
- Standard working hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Lunch break: One hour

Some teams may have different schedules based on customer requirements, project requirements, operational responsibilities, or approved business arrangements.

### 5.2 Employee Availability

Employees are expected to be available during their assigned working hours.

Employees working remotely should maintain the same level of availability and communication expected from office-based employees.

Employees should inform their manager or appropriate team contact if they are unexpectedly unavailable during working hours.

### 5.3 Team-Specific Schedules

Certain teams may operate outside standard business hours.

Examples include:

- Cloud operations
- Infrastructure operations
- Customer support
- Application support
- Projects requiring international time-zone coverage

Team-specific schedules should be communicated by the relevant manager.

### 5.4 Meetings During Working Hours

Employees should make reasonable efforts to attend meetings that are relevant to their responsibilities.

If an employee cannot attend an important meeting, they should inform the meeting organizer when practical.
