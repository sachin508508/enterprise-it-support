# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

# IT FAQ & Quick Reference

## 1. IT Support

### Question

Who should I contact for an IT issue?

### Answer

Employees should contact the Nexora IT Service Desk for IT-related assistance.

**Email:** it-support@nexora.example

## 2. IT Support Hours

### Question

What are the standard IT support hours?

### Answer

The standard IT support operating period follows Nexora's normal business schedule.

Standard business hours are:

- Monday to Friday
- 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Certain operational teams may provide support outside standard hours based on business requirements.

## 3. Reporting an IT Incident

### Question

What information should I provide when reporting an IT incident?

### Answer

Provide:

- Employee name
- Employee ID
- Department
- Device or asset information
- Application or service affected
- Description of the issue
- Error message
- Date and time
- Troubleshooting already attempted

## 4. IT Incident Priority

### Question

What are the IT incident priority levels?

### Answer

IT incidents may be classified as:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

Priority is determined based on factors such as business impact, urgency, number of users affected, and service criticality.

## 5. Critical Incident

### Question

What is a critical IT incident?

### Answer

A critical incident affects a major business service or prevents a significant number of employees from performing essential work.

Examples include:

- Major company-wide service outage
- Critical business application unavailable
- Major network outage

## 6. IT Request

### Question

What happens after I submit an IT request?

### Answer

After submission:

1. The request is recorded.
2. IT reviews the request.
3. Missing information may be requested.
4. Required approvals are obtained.
5. IT processes the request.
6. Status updates may be provided.
7. The request is completed after the required service is provided.

## 7. Existing IT Request

### Question

How do I check my existing IT request?

### Answer

Use the approved IT service-management or ticketing system and use the request or ticket reference provided when the request was submitted.

If the request cannot be located, contact the IT Service Desk.

## 8. IT Request Priority

### Question

How is an IT request prioritized?

### Answer

IT may consider:

- Business impact
- Urgency
- Number of users affected
- Business deadline
- Service criticality
- Required approvals

The priority may change after IT reviews the request.

## 9. Escalating an IT Issue

### Question

How do I escalate an unresolved IT issue?

### Answer

Provide the IT Service Desk with:

- Existing ticket or request reference
- Description of the unresolved problem
- Business impact
- Troubleshooting already attempted
- Current error or status

The IT Service Desk can determine the appropriate escalation path.

## 10. Suspicious Email

### Question

What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?

### Answer

Do not click suspicious links, open unexpected attachments, or provide credentials.

Report the suspicious email through the company's approved security or IT reporting process.

## 11. Unexpected MFA Request

### Question

What should I do if I receive an MFA request that I did not initiate?

### Answer

Do not approve the request.

Report the unexpected MFA request through the approved IT or security support process.

## 12. Lost Company Equipment

### Question

What should I do if company equipment is lost?

### Answer

Report the loss to the appropriate IT or security support channel as soon as possible.

Provide:

- Employee name
- Employee ID
- Device type
- Device asset ID, if available
- Approximate time of loss
- Relevant circumstances

## 13. Stolen Company Equipment

### Question

What should I do if company equipment is stolen?

### Answer

Report the theft immediately through the appropriate IT and security channels.

Provide the device information and relevant circumstances.

## 14. Account Compromise

### Question

What should I do if I think my company account has been compromised?

### Answer

Contact the IT Service Desk or appropriate security channel immediately.

Do not share your password with anyone.

## 15. Password

### Question

Should I share my company password with IT Support?

### Answer

No.

Employees should never share their company password with IT Support, managers, colleagues, or other people.

## 16. Software Installation

### Question

Can I install any software on my company laptop?

### Answer

No.

Employees should use approved software and follow the company's IT request process when software is required for business purposes.
