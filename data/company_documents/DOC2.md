# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

 # Account & IT Access Guide

## 1. Employee Account Creation

### Purpose

Every Nexora employee requiring company technology resources receives an appropriate company account.

### Account Creation

Employee accounts are created as part of the approved onboarding process.

The account setup may include:

- Company username
- Company email account
- Initial authentication setup
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Required application access
- Approved system access

### Required Information

Account creation may require:

- Employee name
- Employee ID
- Department
- Job role
- Manager
- Joining date

Employees should provide accurate information during onboarding.

## 2. Login Credentials

### Username

Employees should use their assigned company username when accessing company systems.

Employees should not share their username or credentials with other people.

### Password

Employees must use a secure password for company accounts.

Passwords should:

- Be difficult for others to guess.
- Not be shared with colleagues.
- Not be reused for unauthorized personal services.
- Be changed when required by company security procedures.

### Password Protection

Employees should never send their password through email, chat, or other communication channels.

IT Support will not ask employees to disclose their password.

## 3. Password Reset

### When a Password Reset Is Required

A password reset may be required when:

- The password is forgotten.
- The password has expired.
- The account is locked.
- A security concern requires a password change.

### Password Reset Process

1. Use the approved company password-reset process.
2. Verify your identity when requested.
3. Create a new secure password.
4. Sign in again using the new password.
5. Complete MFA if requested.

### Security Requirement

Employees should immediately report suspected credential compromise to the appropriate IT or security support channel.

## 4. Multi-Factor Authentication

### Purpose

Multi-Factor Authentication provides an additional security layer when accessing company systems.

### MFA Enrollment

Employees should enroll their approved authentication method during account setup.

The approved method may use:

- Authentication application
- Verification code
- Approved security device
- Other company-approved authentication method

### MFA Usage

Employees should complete MFA when prompted during authentication.

Employees should never approve an authentication request they did not initiate.

### Lost MFA Device

If the registered MFA device is lost or unavailable, contact the IT Service Desk for assistance.

## 5. Email Account Setup

### Company Email

Employees receive a company email account when required for their role.

The company email account is used for approved business communication.

### Email Access

Employees may access company email through approved company applications or web services.

### Email Security

Employees should:

- Protect company email credentials.
- Avoid sharing account information.
- Be careful with unexpected attachments and links.
- Report suspicious messages through the appropriate company process.

## 6. Application Access

### Purpose

Employees may require access to approved business applications based on their role and responsibilities.

### Access Requirements

Application access may depend on:

- Employee role
- Department
- Business requirement
- Manager approval
- Application owner approval
- Security requirements

### Application Access Process

1. Identify the required application.
2. Provide the business reason.
3. Submit the approved access request.
4. Obtain required approval.
5. IT or the application owner reviews the request.
6. Access is provided after approval.

## 7. Shared Drive Access

### Purpose

Shared drives provide access to approved company files and team resources.

### Access Requirements

Shared-drive access should be based on an employee's job responsibilities.

### Request Process

1. Identify the required shared drive.
2. Provide the business reason.
3. Specify the required access level.
4. Obtain manager or owner approval when required.
5. Access is provisioned after approval.

### Access Levels

Depending on the resource, access may include:

- Read
- Edit
- Create
- Manage

Employees should only receive the level of access required for their work.

## 8. VPN Access

### Purpose

VPN access provides secure remote connectivity to approved company resources.

### Eligibility

VPN access may be provided when required for an employee's job responsibilities.

Eligibility may depend on:

- Employee role
- Business requirement
- Manager approval
- Security requirements
- Remote-working requirements

### VPN Access Process

1. Identify the required remote resources.
2. Provide the business reason.
3. Submit the approved VPN access request.
4. Obtain required approval.
5. Complete security requirements.
6. IT enables the approved VPN access.
7. Employee receives connection instructions.

### VPN Security

Employees must use VPN access only for approved company activities.

VPN credentials should not be shared with other people.

## 9. Access Changes After Department Transfer

When an employee moves to another department or changes roles, their system access may need to be updated.

### Access Change Process

1. Employee or manager reports the role change.
2. Required access is reviewed.
3. Unnecessary access may be removed.
4. New access requirements are identified.
5. Required approvals are obtained.
6. Approved access is provisioned.

Employees should not retain access that is no longer required for their role.

## 10. Access Removal During Offboarding

When an employee leaves Nexora, company account and system access must be removed according to the company's offboarding process.

### Offboarding Activities

Depending on the employee's role, offboarding may include:

- Account deactivation
- Email access removal
- Application access removal
- Shared-drive access removal
- VPN access removal
- Company device collection
- Credential or access review

Access removal is coordinated with the appropriate company teams.

## 11. Software and License Access

Employees may require access to approved software or licensed applications.

### Requirements

Software access may depend on:

- Business need
- Employee role
- License availability
- Manager approval
- Security requirements

### Process

1. Identify the required software.
2. Provide the business purpose.
3. Submit the approved request.
4. Obtain required approval.
5. IT checks license availability.
6. Access is provided if approved.

Employees should not use unauthorized software or licenses.

## 12. Access Security Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for protecting their company access.

Employees should:

- Keep credentials confidential.
- Use MFA when required.
- Lock their computer when away.
- Avoid sharing accounts.
- Report suspicious authentication activity.
- Report lost devices promptly.
- Use only approved company systems.

## 13. Account Security Incidents

Employees should immediately report:

- Suspected account compromise
- Unexpected login notifications
- Unexpected MFA requests
- Lost authentication devices
- Lost company devices
- Suspected credential theft

Employees should not attempt to investigate security incidents independently if doing so could create additional risk.

## 14. Account Access Contact

For account or access assistance, employees should contact the Nexora IT Service Desk.
