# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

# IT Services & Request Catalog

## 1. Available IT Services

Nexora IT provides the following services:

- New laptop requests
- Laptop replacement
- Software installation
- Software and license requests
- New application requests
- Hardware requests
- Email and mailbox requests
- Network service requests
- VPN service requests
- Access requests

The availability of a service may depend on the employee's role, department, business requirements, approvals, and resource availability.

## 2. New Laptop Request

### Service Description

Employees may request a company laptop when a new device is required for their job responsibilities.

### Required Information

A laptop request should include:

- Employee name
- Employee ID
- Department
- Job role
- Manager
- Business reason
- Required date
- Location
- Required specifications, if applicable

### Request Process

1. Submit a laptop request through the approved IT service process.
2. Provide the required employee and business information.
3. Obtain manager approval if required.
4. IT reviews the request.
5. IT confirms the appropriate device.
6. The laptop is prepared and assigned.

### Processing Time

Processing depends on device availability, business priority, approvals, and IT workload.

## 3. Laptop Replacement

### Service Description

Employees may request a replacement when an existing company laptop requires replacement.

### Replacement Reasons

A replacement may be considered when:

- The device reaches the end of its supported lifecycle.
- The device has a hardware failure.
- The device no longer meets business requirements.
- The device has been damaged.

### Required Information

- Employee name
- Employee ID
- Device asset ID
- Department
- Manager
- Reason for replacement
- Description of the device problem

### Request Process

1. Submit a laptop replacement request.
2. Provide the device asset information.
3. Explain the reason for replacement.
4. IT reviews the device information.
5. IT determines the appropriate action.
6. Replacement is scheduled if approved.

## 12. Request Processing Time

Processing time depends on:

- Request type
- Business priority
- Required approvals
- Hardware availability
- Software license availability
- Security review requirements
- IT workload
- Number of users affected

Standard requests are normally processed through the standard IT service process.

Complex requests may require additional technical, security, or management review.

## 13. Required Information for IT Requests

Employees should provide complete and accurate information.

Common information includes:

- Employee name
- Employee ID
- Department
- Manager
- Request type
- Business purpose
- Required service
- Required date
- Location
- Number of users
- Required access level
- Supporting information

Incomplete requests may require additional clarification.

## 14. Request Priority

### Critical

A critical request is associated with a major business requirement or significant operational impact.

### High

A high-priority request has significant business impact or a short business deadline.

### Medium

A medium-priority request supports normal business activities without immediate operational impact.

### Low

A low-priority request has limited business impact and can be handled through normal scheduling.

IT may change the priority after reviewing the actual business impact.

## 15. Request Approval

Depending on the request, approval may be required from:

- Employee's manager
- Department head
- Application owner
- IT team
- Security team
- Finance or procurement team

The required approval depends on the type and sensitivity of the requested service.

## 16. After Submitting a Request

After submitting an IT service request:

1. The request is recorded.
2. IT reviews the submitted information.
3. Missing information may be requested.
4. Required approvals are obtained.
5. IT processes the request.
6. Status updates may be provided.
7. The service is provided after approval and processing.
8. Employee may be asked to confirm completion.

Employees should retain the request or ticket reference.

## 17. Request Cancellation or Change

If an employee no longer requires a submitted request, they should contact the IT Service Desk and provide the request reference.

If the requested service needs to be changed, the employee should provide the updated requirements.

IT will determine whether the request can be modified or whether a new request is required.
