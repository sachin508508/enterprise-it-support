# Nexora Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

# IT Troubleshooting & Incident Guide


## 1. VPN Connection Failure

### Symptoms

- VPN does not connect.
- VPN disconnects unexpectedly.
- VPN shows a connection error.
- Company resources cannot be accessed through VPN.

### Possible Causes

- Internet connectivity problem.
- Incorrect credentials.
- MFA verification failure.
- VPN client problem.
- Temporary VPN service interruption.
- Network restriction.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Check the internet connection.
2. Restart the VPN application.
3. Verify the company credentials.
4. Complete MFA verification.
5. Restart the computer.
6. Try connecting again.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- VPN still does not connect.
- Multiple employees have the same problem.
- Authentication continues to fail.
- Company resources remain unavailable.

## 2. Wi-Fi Connection Failure

### Symptoms

- Device cannot connect to Wi-Fi.
- Wi-Fi repeatedly disconnects.
- Internet connection is slow.
- Company Wi-Fi cannot be detected.

### Possible Causes

- Wi-Fi is disabled.
- Incorrect network selected.
- Weak wireless signal.
- Temporary network interruption.
- Device network configuration problem.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Check that Wi-Fi is enabled.
2. Select the approved company Wi-Fi network.
3. Disconnect and reconnect.
4. Move closer to the wireless access point.
5. Restart the computer.
6. Try connecting again.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- Wi-Fi remains unavailable.
- Multiple employees are affected.
- Company Wi-Fi cannot be detected.

## 3. Laptop Running Slowly

### Symptoms

- Applications take a long time to open.
- Computer freezes temporarily.
- Files take longer to open.
- Applications become unresponsive.

### Possible Causes

- Too many applications are running.
- Low available storage.
- Pending system updates.
- High system resource usage.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Close unnecessary applications.
2. Close unnecessary browser tabs.
3. Check available storage.
4. Restart the laptop.
5. Install approved system updates.
6. Try the application again.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- The laptop remains slow after restarting.
- Applications repeatedly become unresponsive.
- The system frequently freezes.

## 4. Laptop Will Not Start

### Symptoms

- Laptop does not power on.
- Screen remains blank.
- Laptop shuts down immediately.
- Laptop does not reach the login screen.

### Possible Causes

- Battery is discharged.
- Power adapter problem.
- Power connection problem.
- Hardware failure.
- Operating-system startup problem.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Connect the approved power adapter.
2. Confirm that the adapter is connected correctly.
3. Allow the laptop to charge.
4. Try turning the laptop on again.
5. Disconnect unnecessary external devices.
6. Try starting the laptop again.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- Laptop does not power on after charging.
- Screen remains blank.
- Hardware errors appear.
- There is visible physical damage.

Employees should not attempt to repair company-owned hardware themselves.

## 5. Email Not Syncing

### Symptoms

- New emails are not appearing.
- Sent messages remain pending.
- Email synchronization stops.
- Email application displays an error.

### Possible Causes

- Internet connectivity problem.
- Email application synchronization issue.
- Account authentication problem.
- Temporary email service issue.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Check the internet connection.
2. Open the approved company email service.
3. Check whether new messages appear.
4. Restart the email application.
5. Restart the computer.
6. Verify that the company account is signed in.
7. Complete MFA verification if requested.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- Email remains unsynchronized.
- Web email cannot be accessed.
- Authentication repeatedly fails.
- Multiple employees experience the same problem.

## 6. MFA Not Working

### Symptoms

- MFA approval does not arrive.
- Verification code is rejected.
- Authentication application does not respond.
- Employee cannot complete authentication.

### Possible Causes

- Device connectivity problem.
- Authentication application issue.
- Incorrect verification code.
- Registered MFA device is unavailable.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Check the MFA device network connection.
2. Open the authentication application.
3. Verify the authentication method.
4. Enter the latest verification code if required.
5. Restart the authentication application.
6. Try authentication again.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- The registered MFA device is lost.
- The device is unavailable.
- MFA repeatedly fails.
- Employee cannot access the company account.

Do not approve unexpected MFA requests.

## 7. Printer Not Working

### Symptoms

- Document does not print.
- Print job remains in the queue.
- Printer is unavailable.
- Computer cannot find the printer.

### Possible Causes

- Printer is offline.
- Printer has no paper.
- Print queue is blocked.
- Incorrect printer selected.
- Network connection problem.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Confirm that the correct printer is selected.
2. Check that the printer is powered on.
3. Check the printer display for errors.
4. Confirm that paper is available.
5. Check whether the printer is online.
6. Try printing the document again.
7. Restart the computer if necessary.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- Printer remains offline.
- Multiple employees cannot print.
- Printer displays a hardware error.
- Print jobs repeatedly fail.

## 8. Application Crashing

### Symptoms

- Application closes unexpectedly.
- Application freezes.
- Application becomes unresponsive.
- Application displays an error message.

### Possible Causes

- Application software issue.
- Outdated application.
- Operating-system compatibility issue.
- Insufficient system resources.
- Temporary service issue.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Save work if possible.
2. Close the affected application.
3. Reopen the application.
4. Try the same operation again.
5. Restart the computer if necessary.
6. Check for approved application updates.
7. Record any error message.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- Application continues to crash.
- Multiple employees experience the same issue.
- Important business work cannot be completed.
- A critical error is displayed.

## 9. Internet Connection Failure

### Symptoms

- Websites do not load.
- Internet connection is unavailable.
- Connection repeatedly drops.
- Online applications cannot connect.

### Possible Causes

- Wi-Fi connectivity problem.
- Network service interruption.
- Device network configuration problem.
- Router or access-point problem.

### Troubleshooting Steps

1. Check whether the device is connected to the network.
2. Try accessing another website.
3. Disconnect and reconnect to the network.
4. Restart the computer.
5. Check whether other employees are affected.
6. If working remotely, check the local internet connection.
7. Try accessing the required service again.

### When to Escalate

Contact the IT Service Desk if:

- Internet remains unavailable.
- Multiple employees are affected.
- Company applications cannot connect.
- A network outage is suspected.

## 10. Incident Reporting

### Information to Provide

When reporting an IT problem, provide:

- Employee name
- Employee ID
- Department
- Device name or asset ID
- Application or service affected
- Description of the problem
- Date and time of the issue
- Error message
- Screenshot if available
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted

### Reporting Process

1. Identify the problem.
2. Perform the relevant troubleshooting steps.
3. Record the results.
4. Contact the IT Service Desk if the problem continues.
5. Provide the troubleshooting information.

## 11. Incident Priority

### Critical

A critical incident affects a major business service or a significant number of employees.

Examples:

- Company-wide service outage
- Critical business application unavailable
- Major network outage

### High

A high-priority incident significantly affects an employee, team, or important business process.

Examples:

- Important business application unavailable
- Major connectivity problem
- Critical access problem

### Medium

A medium-priority incident affects normal work but a temporary workaround may be available.

Examples:

- Individual application problem
- Email synchronization problem
- Printer problem

### Low

A low-priority incident has limited business impact.

Examples:

- Minor application issue
- Non-urgent configuration problem
- General IT assistance

## 12. Immediate IT Support

Employees should contact IT Support promptly when:

- A critical company service is unavailable.
- A critical business activity cannot continue.
- A company device is lost or stolen.
- An account may have been compromised.
- Unexpected MFA requests are received.
- A security incident is suspected.
- Multiple employees are affected by the same issue.

## 13. General Troubleshooting Process

1. Identify the problem.
2. Check the symptoms.
3. Check basic connectivity.
4. Restart the affected application.
5. Restart the device if required.
6. Try the operation again.
7. Contact IT Support if the issue remains unresolved.

Employees should not perform unauthorized system changes or hardware repairs.
